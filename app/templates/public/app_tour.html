How It Works

Your Journey to Better Results

Smart Study is not just a tool — it's a structured academic coaching experience. Here's how it guides you from confusion to clarity.

1
Understand Yourself

Complete your academic profile: your study level, program, subjects, attendance, and target score. Smart Study uses this to personalise everything that follows.

2
Discover Your Strengths & Weaknesses

Take an adaptive assessment that adjusts question difficulty based on your answers. The result is an accurate picture of your current knowledge — per subject, per topic.

3
Identify What Needs Attention

Smart Study analyses your performance and flags subjects at academic risk. You see clear, readable risk indicators — not raw scores — so you know exactly where to focus.

4
Build Your Study Plan

Generate a personalised weekly study schedule. High-risk subjects automatically get more time. Your availability and exam timeline are factored in automatically.

5
Study & Practice

Follow your plan, access curated study materials for each subject, take chapter quizzes, and ask your AI Study Coach when you're stuck on a topic.

6
Track Your Progress

Log every study session and watch your consistency build over time. See your weekly hours, session streaks, and total time invested.

7
Improve

Re-run your risk analysis after studying. Retake assessments to measure improvement. As your performance improves, your study plan adapts accordingly.
