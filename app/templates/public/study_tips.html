Evidence-Based Study Tips

Techniques that pair well with your Smart Study planner and ML insights.

Pomodoro Technique

25 minutes focused study + 5 minute break. Matches the 2-hour blocks in your adaptive planner.

Spaced Repetition

Review material at increasing intervals. Log sessions in Study Log to track consistency.

Sleep & Memory

Memory consolidation happens during sleep. All-nighters hurt recall — plan rest days in your schedule.

Active Recall

Test yourself instead of re-reading. Use the adaptive assessment to identify weak subjects.

Prioritize High-Risk Subjects

When ML flags a subject as high risk, allocate extra hours that week — the planner does this automatically.

Track & Adjust

Re-run ML evaluation after midterms. Update your planner when grades or attendance change.
