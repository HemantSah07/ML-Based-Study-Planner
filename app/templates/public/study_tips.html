Evidence-Based Techniques

Study Tips That Actually Work

Practical techniques used by high-performing students — designed to pair with your Smart Study plan and Risk Insights.

{# ── Focus ── #}
🎯 Focus & Concentration
Pomodoro Technique

Study for 25 minutes, take a 5-minute break. After 4 cycles, take a longer 15–30 minute rest. This maintains concentration without mental fatigue.

Eliminate Distractions

Use phone-free, notification-free study blocks. One hour of truly focused study is worth more than three hours of interrupted work.

Dedicated Study Space

Your brain associates places with activities. A consistent, clean study environment helps you shift into focus mode faster.

{# ── Memory ── #}
🧠 Memory & Retention
Spaced Repetition

Review material at increasing intervals: 1 day, 3 days, 1 week, 2 weeks. This method dramatically increases long-term retention.

Active Recall

Test yourself instead of re-reading. Close your notes and try to recall the key concepts. Use Smart Study's assessment to practice this regularly.

Sleep & Memory

Memory consolidation happens during sleep. Studying late at night can help short-term recall but hurts long-term retention. Plan rest days in your schedule.

{# ── Exam Prep ── #}
📝 Exam Preparation
Prioritise High-Risk Subjects

When Smart Study flags a subject as high risk, allocate extra hours that week. Your study planner does this automatically when you set subject priorities.

Past Paper Practice

Practising with past exam papers is one of the most effective exam preparation strategies. It familiarises you with question patterns and timing pressure.

Track & Adapt

Re-run your Risk Insights analysis after midterms or when your grades change. Update your study planner so your schedule stays aligned with your actual performance.

{# ── Healthy Habits ── #}
🌱 Healthy Study Habits
Physical Health Matters

Exercise, hydration, and healthy eating directly affect concentration and memory. Even a 10-minute walk between study sessions improves cognitive performance.

Study Groups

Explaining concepts to others is one of the fastest ways to deepen understanding. Study with peers for subjects where discussion and debate can clarify concepts.

Realistic Goals

Set daily and weekly study targets you can actually maintain. Consistency over weeks beats heroic effort before exams every time.
